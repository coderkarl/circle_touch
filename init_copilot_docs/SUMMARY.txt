HOW IT WORKS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DETECT
  Read 5 line sensors → Values 0-1023
  └─ <400 = Black, >400 = White

MEASURE
  Center of black sensors × 14.4mm spacing
  └─ Calculate lateral offset from perfect alignment

CORRECT
  If cross detected AND within 10cm of target:
  └─ Reset position to target coordinates
  └─ Drift cleared, ready for next waypoint
